Medicaid Policy Search Agent

Sanitized implementation case study

From manual policy search to citation-backed review

A Microsoft Copilot Studio RAG agent covering 1,800+ pages of Medicaid policy reduced routine lookup time from 10-20 minutes to under 30 seconds.

10-20 min → <30 sec Routine policy lookup
1,800+ pages Searchable policy corpus

Problem

Prior authorization reviewers needed to locate precise policy language under service-level deadlines. Manual navigation was slow, and a fast answer without traceable support would have created a different risk.

Constraints

  • Keep the reviewer responsible for the clinical decision.
  • Return citations and risk flags with the answer.
  • Make uncertainty and out-of-scope questions visible.
  • Do not publish policy excerpts, production data, or internal system details.

Approach

I configured a retrieval-augmented search workflow in Microsoft Copilot Studio. The response pattern paired a concise answer with source citations and risk flags so reviewers could verify the result instead of treating the model as an authority.

Outcome

The workflow reduced routine policy lookup from 10-20 minutes to under 30 seconds. The result is an observed operational comparison, not a controlled causal study, and final review remained with the clinician.

Public-proof boundary: This case study contains aggregate, resume-supported outcomes only. It includes no PHI, provider identifiers, policy passages, screenshots, prompts, credentials, production data, or employer-system architecture.
