The evidence behind these claims

Every material claim in the three case studies links to source, tests, a pinned commit, public CI, a release, or a dated live check.

Selection

Why these three

I compared the repositories for employer relevance, implementation depth, completeness, and the strength of their public proof. The selection does not rely on adoption, revenue, or productivity figures.

CandidateDecisionEvidence-based rationale
Ask JDPSelectedThis is a public full-stack AI system. Its live interface, tests, security boundary, and CI are inspectable.
TinkSelectedThis released Rust tool has a public acceptance contract, filesystem refusals, CI, and release artifacts.
Tink SkillsSelectedThis agent-evaluation package has integrity checks, four adapters, tests, and passed CI on published main.
SocratinkNot selectedThe repository says it is not production. Its current evidence is a no-write harness slice and a bounded experiment, not learner outcomes.
Legacy clinical automationsNot selectedThe older portfolio described internal outcomes, but the implementation and production artifacts are not public enough to support an engineering case study.

Background boundary

Where the career facts come from

Roles, dates, operating scope, and education come from the linked public résumé and the reviewed Ask JDP corpus. They are self-reported career records, not independently audited impact evidence. The portfolio identifies me as a registered nurse and makes no claim about current license status.

Limits

Limits of this evidence

  • This portfolio does not report user counts, adoption, revenue, conversion, uptime, productivity, or causal impact.
  • It does not describe Ask JDP as hallucination-free, HIPAA-certified, or security-certified.
  • It does not describe Tink as transactionally atomic, concurrency-safe, Windows-compatible, or compatible with private GitHub repositories.
  • It does not claim that the evaluation loop makes a skill universally better or proves statistical significance.
  • It does not present Socratink as a deployed learning platform or as evidence of learner outcomes.
